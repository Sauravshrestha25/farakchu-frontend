# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in Kathmandu browsing Farakchu apparel on their phone or laptop. They want to understand the point of view behind the clothing, see the current pieces, and start an order through Instagram DM.

## Product Purpose

The apparel page makes Farakchu's limited clothing drops feel like part of a lived-in community, not a generic shop. Success means visitors recognize the brand, explore the available pieces, and message @farakchu.apparel to order.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, local. The voice is direct, warm, and a little irreverent, with the confidence of a real place and a real crowd.

## Anti-references

Do not look like a generic streetwear storefront, a polished luxury catalog, a beige editorial portfolio, or a templated ecommerce grid. Avoid invented social proof, fake scarcity, and claims not supplied by Farakchu.

## Design Principles

- Make the clothing feel connected to the people and place behind it.
- Let one strong visual idea lead each section.
- Keep the route useful: show the pieces, then make ordering obvious.
- Use movement and contrast to make a small drop feel alive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable contrast, meaningful image alt text, keyboard-visible focus states, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Do not rely on color alone to communicate product information.
